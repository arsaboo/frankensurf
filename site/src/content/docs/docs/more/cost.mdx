A scraping API charges for every page it fetches, right or wrong. Frankensurf's free tools cost
nothing per page; you pay for the machine they run on, and for a paid tool only on the pages the
free ones can't get. So the fair number is what each **correct** page costs.

## Per 1,000 correct pages

| | Pages read correctly | Cost per 1,000 pages | Cost per 1,000 correct pages |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Firecrawl, Hobby ($16/mo, 5k pages) | 84.7% | $3.20 | $3.78 |
| Firecrawl, Standard ($83/mo, 100k) | 84.7% | $0.83 | $0.98 |
| Firecrawl, Scale ($599/mo, 1M) | 84.7% | $0.60 | $0.71 |
| **Frankensurf, free tools**, on a rented 4 vCPU VM | 88.0% | $0.21 | **$0.24** |
| **Frankensurf, free tools**, on a machine you already have | 88.0% | $0 | **$0** |
| **Frankensurf with paid fallbacks** (Firecrawl at the Standard rate) | 96.0% | $0.41 | **$0.43** |

So free tools read more pages correctly than Firecrawl alone for about a third of its best rate,
and with paid fallbacks Frankensurf costs less than Firecrawl's Scale plan while reading 96% of
pages instead of 85%.

## Where the numbers come from

- **Pages read correctly:** held-out set 7, 150 sites picked before any was read, every tool alone
  and Frankensurf, the same day, graded by the same check
  ([rows](https://github.com/yail259/frankensurf/tree/main/benchmarks/2026-10-11)).
- **Firecrawl:** its pricing page on 16 October 2026, billed yearly: 1 credit for a basic page
  scrape. A page that comes back 403 or 404 still costs a credit; JSON extraction adds 4 credits a
  page; monthly credits don't roll over below Scale.
- **Frankensurf, free tools, compute:** the same 150 sites read in free mode on a GitHub-hosted
  runner (4 vCPU, 16 GB), four at a time: 2,333 seconds of reading, about 925 pages an hour. Priced
  at AWS's on-demand rate for a comparable machine (m6i.xlarge, $0.192 an hour), that is $0.21 per
  1,000 pages; cheaper hosts, spot capacity or a machine you already run bring it down to cents or
  nothing. One run; more reads at a time may fit on the same machine.
- **With paid fallbacks:** 37 of the 150 reads cost anything, each one Firecrawl credit spent on a
  page the free tools couldn't get (29 of them got the page through Firecrawl). At Firecrawl's
  Standard rate that is $0.20 per 1,000 pages, plus the same compute.

## At a million pages a month

| | Monthly cost | Correct pages |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Firecrawl Scale | $599 | about 847,000 |
| Frankensurf, free tools, rented VMs | about $210 (two VMs) | about 880,000 |
| Frankensurf with paid fallbacks | about $545 (two VMs, and Firecrawl Growth for about 247,000 fallback credits) | about 960,000 |

<Aside type="note" title="What this leaves out">
  Firecrawl runs the machines for you; Frankensurf runs on yours, so someone looks after them.
  Frankensurf is also slower on walled pages (a median of 3 to 9 seconds a page depending on the
  machine, against Firecrawl's 4.6), so a big job needs more reads at a time. And these are basic
  page reads: Firecrawl's extraction and Frankensurf's structured fields aren't priced the same way.
</Aside>
