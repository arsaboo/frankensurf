Two benchmarks back the numbers on the landing page. Both judge every read by
its content, not its status code: a product page must have a price, a search page
must have result links, an article must have real text. A challenge page or an
empty app shell counts as a failure.

## Fresh sites

The fairest test: lists of sites picked before any of their pages was read,
used once. Nothing was tuned on them.

**Newest: 150 more sites, 11 October 2026, read once (held-out set 7), every
tool alone.** The candidates proposed for set 6 that set 6 did not take, never
read before. Read from a home connection; Firecrawl, Zyte and Jina Reader fetch
from their own networks.

| Tool | Sites read | Median | p90 | Cost per 1k pages |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Patchright only | 38.7% | 4.1 s | 8.3 s | free |
| Headless Chromium only | 40.7% | 2.7 s | 6.8 s | free |
| Plain HTTP only | 48.7% | 1.2 s | 3.3 s | free |
| Camoufox only | 68.7% | 10.9 s | 15.7 s | free |
| Scrapling only | 68.7% | 8.5 s | 19.2 s | free |
| Jina Reader only | 73.3% | 1.5 s | 10.2 s | free |
| Firecrawl only | 84.7% | 4.6 s | 10.6 s | $4.28 |
| Zyte only | 86.0% | 10.7 s | 31.3 s | not reported |
| Frankensurf, free tools only | 88.0% | 3.6 s | 21.3 s | $0 |
| **Frankensurf, with paid fallbacks** | **96.0%** | 2.9 s | 25.7 s | $0.98 |

The free tools alone read as many sites as the best paid tool on its own: 16
sites only the free tools read against 11 only Firecrawl read, a gap within the
noise of 150 sites (on set 6 it went the other way, 84.7% against 85.3%). With paid
fallbacks, Frankensurf read 17 sites Firecrawl missed and Firecrawl none it
missed; of the 146 sites any tool read, it got 98.6%. From a fresh cloud VM
(v0.35): free tools 82.0%, [lightning mode](/docs/read/lightning/) 84.0% at a
1.2 s median. Rows:
[`benchmarks/2026-10-11/`](https://github.com/yail259/frankensurf/tree/main/benchmarks/2026-10-11).

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Plain HTTP only | 51.3% | 1.0 s | 3.0 s | free |
| Camoufox only | 66.0% | 8.5 s | 13.3 s | free |
| Scrapling only | 72.7% | 6.8 s | 17.2 s | free |
| Jina Reader only | 74.0% | 2.3 s | 10.4 s | free |

| **Frankensurf, with paid fallbacks** | **95.3%** | 3.0 s | 25.9 s | $1.06 |

Times are over pages read; cost is per 1,000 pages read right. Frankensurf with
paid fallbacks read 18 sites Firecrawl missed, and Firecrawl none that it
missed; of the 146 sites any tool read, it got 97.9%. Its median read is the
fastest of the tools that read most pages, but its slowest tenth is slower than
Firecrawl's. ZenRows and Scrapfly are left out: their plans had run out of
credits. The same set read from a fresh cloud VM (a data-centre address) with
v0.33: free tools 82.7% (v0.32: 78.7%), [lightning mode](/docs/read/lightning/)
84.0% at a 1.6 s median.

Re-run with v0.34 the next morning: with paid fallbacks 95.3% again (median
3.0 s, p90 25.9 s; the three reads that took 90 seconds or more now take 21 to
43); free tools 84.0%, median 4.4 s. The free median moves with Jina Reader's
cache: its first read of a page took a median 5.8 s that morning, and the
first run above came an hour after other runs had read the same pages through
it.

v0.33 against v0.32 on 40 sites of the v0.30 set below (10 October), one read at a time, both
versions on every site: free tools read 37 against 35 (median 3.9 s against
6.5 s); with paid fallbacks 37 against 36 (median 3.6 s against 5.1 s, $1.32
against $0.83 per 1,000 pages: paid tools now come in at the first wall). Of 7
URLs that answered not found, 4 came back as the site's own search results for
what they asked for ([When the URL is wrong](/docs/blocked/escalation/#when-the-url-is-wrong)).

## Search

The same idea for `search`: queries written before any was run, used once,
every search tool on its own and Frankensurf. A result counts as right only
when it is about the query, inside the date window the query asks for, and an
actual item, not a homepage, listing or challenge page.

**120 queries, 12 October 2026, v0.39.0 (blind set 4):** news this week,
company announcements, how-tos, code, papers and facts, 20 of each.

| Tool | Queries with a right result in the top 5 | Right results per query (of 5) | Share of returned results right | Cost per 1k searches |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Exa Only | 100% | 4.68 | 93.7% | $9.50 |
| Frankensurf, paid allowed | 100% | 4.53 | 97.4% | $0.76 |
| **Frankensurf, free** | **100%** | **4.36** | **95.8%** | **free** |
| Firecrawl Search Only | 99.2% | 3.54 | 70.8% | $11.40 |
| SearXNG Only | 82.5% | 2.92 | 58.5% | free |
| Bing News RSS Only | 74.2% | 2.38 | 64.4% | free |
| Wikipedia Only | 49.2% | 1.97 | 41.5% | free |
| arXiv Only | 39.2% | 1.53 | 37.7% | free |
| Bing RSS Only | 39.2% | 1.39 | 27.8% | free |
| GitHub Only | 33.3% | 1.31 | 31.3% | free |
| Stack Exchange Only | 28.3% | 1.21 | 24.6% | free |
| Hacker News Only | 27.5% | 0.85 | 22.0% | free |
| Open Library Only | 13.3% | 0.28 | 8.6% | free |
| DuckDuckGo Only | 0% (CAPTCHA every time) | 0 | 0% | free |

Paired, query by query (sign test):

- Frankensurf free against Firecrawl Search Only: more right results on 53 queries, fewer on 22 (p = 0.0004); higher precision on 65, lower on 8 (p < 0.0001).
- Frankensurf free against Exa Only: Exa returns more right results on 33 queries, Frankensurf on 19 (p = 0.07, not significant); Frankensurf has higher precision on 24, lower on 10 (p = 0.02), partly because it returns fewer results when it cannot verify a date (4.56 per query against 5.0).
- Frankensurf free against SearXNG Only, the best free engine on its own: more right results on 64 queries, fewer on 6 (p < 0.0001).

By kind, Frankensurf free against Exa Only, right results per query: announcements 2.7 against 4.7 (Frankensurf returns 3.1 results; the rest it cannot date), news 4.2 against 4.7, how-to 4.8 against 4.8, code 4.7 against 4.2, papers 4.9 against 5.0, facts 4.8 against 4.8.

Raw rows, the queries and the script: [benchmarks/2026-10-12-search](https://github.com/yail259/frankensurf/tree/main/benchmarks/2026-10-12-search).
Run it yourself with `python scripts/searchbench.py --set blind4` (add `--paid`
for Exa and Firecrawl).

## Tool benchmark

Every tool reads the same pages on its own, then the stitched router reads them
too. **Tuned** is 78 pages from the site benchmark, which route seeds were
built on. **Unseen** is 45 sites in two held-out lists that nothing was tuned
on. Measured 6 October 2026.

| Arm | Tuned (78) | Unseen (45) | $ per 1k valid pages, unseen |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Headless Chromium | 32.1% | 15.6% | $0 |
| Plain HTTP | 43.6% | 33.3% | $0 |
| Jina Reader | 35.9% | 51.1% | $0 |
| Camoufox | 60.3% | 48.9% | $0 |
| Scrapling | 61.5% | 60.0% | $0 |
| Firecrawl | 88.5% | 73.3% | $4.61 |
| Frankensurf, free tools only | 89.7% | 53.3% | $0 |
| **Frankensurf** | **98.7%** | **71.1%** | **$1.07** |

- On tuned sites, stitching beats every single tool.
- On unseen sites it is 2 points behind Firecrawl, at a quarter of the cost,
  and slower: median 6.6 s against 2.8 s.
- Some single tool read 89% of the unseen pages. The gap is the router's
  choices: 11 of its 13 unseen misses were pages that looked fine but lacked
  their content.

ZenRows is left out: the plan's rate limit rejected its first reads, so its
numbers say more about the account than about ZenRows.

```bash
PYTHONPATH=.:src python scripts/toolbench.py --state /tmp/tb \
  --rows benchmarks/2026-10-06/sitebench-paid-v0.14.jsonl
```

Raw rows and the summary are in
[`benchmarks/2026-10-06/`](https://github.com/yail259/frankensurf/tree/main/benchmarks/2026-10-06).

## Site benchmark

`scripts/sitebench.py` reads real deep pages (product pages, search results,
articles, PDFs, JSON) behind named walls, and follows search results to the
pages they link to.

| Version | Arm | Valid content | False successes | p50 | Paid cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| v0.11.0 | free | 61/72 (85%) | 4 | 3.0 s | $0 |
| v0.11.0 | paid | 67/72 (93%) | 4 | 3.5 s | $0.07 |
| v0.12.0 | paid | 77/82 | 4 | 3.5 s | $0.033 |
| v0.13.0 | paid | 82/82 | 0 | 4.1 s | $0.029 |
| v0.14.0 | paid | 82/82 | 0 | 4.4 s | $0.015 |

```bash
PYTHONPATH=.:src python scripts/sitebench.py --state /tmp/sb-1          # free
PYTHONPATH=.:src python scripts/sitebench.py --state /tmp/sb-2 --paid   # paid fallbacks
```

<Aside type="tip">
  Use a fresh `--state` for every run. One run's blocks would otherwise cool
  down sites for the next.
</Aside>
